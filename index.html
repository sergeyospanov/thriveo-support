About Thriveo

Thriveo is an iPhone app focused on the balance of the day.

The app helps you stay aware of both positive and negative activities, return to plus after setbacks, and keep your rhythm over time.

The current release works without account creation or login.

FAQ

  • Do I need an account? No. The current version works without registration or login.
  • Where is my data stored? The current release stores app data locally on your device.
  • Are notifications required? No. Notifications are optional and can be enabled or disabled in the app.
